doom|1993|id Software|A marine stationed on a moon of Mars is the last one standing after a teleporter experiment lets Hell through. He fights out of the bases on Phobos and Deimos and on into Hell itself, with the shotgun, the chaingun and the BFG 9000. The game that defined the first-person shooter.
sigil|2019|John Romero|John Romero's return to Doom, a quarter of a century after he designed many of its first maps. Nine new levels as a fifth episode, set after Doom's ending, pull the marine back into a Hell twisted by Baphomet's sigil. Tight, demanding maps in Romero's style, made for the original game.
sigil2|2023|John Romero|A sixth episode for Doom by John Romero, released for the game's thirtieth anniversary. Nine more levels pick up after SIGIL, deeper in Hell and harder still, with big set pieces built within the limits of the original engine.
doom2|1994|id Software|The invasion has reached Earth. The one marine who made it back from Mars fights through starports, a ruined city and finally Hell itself, to close the portal from the other side. Thirty-two levels, the super shotgun and demons such as the Arch-vile: the sequel every shooter after it was measured against.
nerve|2010|Nerve Software|Nine levels made for the Xbox 360 release of Doom II. The marine goes back to Hell to finish what the demons started, through bases and ruins designed in the spirit of id's own maps, with a secret level hidden among them.
master|1995|id Software and others|Twenty-one levels for Doom II that id chose from designers outside the company, among them Tom Mustaine, Sverre Kvernmo and John Anderson. Each is a mission of its own, started on its own, from cramped fortresses to sprawling battles in the open.
tnt|1996|TeamTNT|Thirty-two new levels for Doom II by TeamTNT, a group of fans whose work id published as one half of Final Doom. The demons have broken through again at a UAC research base, and the fight leads through labs, warehouses, pyramids and the Evilution's own Hell.
plutonia|1996|Dario and Milo Casali|The other half of Final Doom: thirty-two levels by the brothers Dario and Milo Casali, built for players who had mastered Doom II. Revenants and Arch-viles everywhere, traps behind every switch, jungle temples and a gate the demons opened with the UAC's own experiment.
heretic|1994|Raven Software|Raven Software's dark fantasy on the Doom engine. D'Sparil, the third of the Serpent Riders, rules the land, and Corvus, an elf who refused to serve him, takes him on with a wand, a crossbow and a phoenix rod. The Tome of Power makes every weapon stronger for a while.
hexen|1995|Raven Software|Korax, the second Serpent Rider, has taken Cronos. Three heroes stand against him: a fighter with fists and an axe, a cleric with a mace and a serpent staff, and a mage who throws lightning, through hubs of maps linked to each other, with switches to find and puzzles that span them.
hexdd|1996|Raven Software|Deathkings of the Dark Citadel, the expansion for Hexen. Three new hubs of linked maps take any of the three heroes through castles, ruins and the citadel of the Deathkings, with harder puzzles and harder battles than the game before it.
strife|1996|Rogue Entertainment|A shooter with a story, on the Doom engine. A mercenary joins the Front, a resistance against the Order, a cult that rules the world since a comet fell. He talks to people, takes on missions, buys weapons in town and chooses sides, in a world of linked maps that was unusual for its time.
